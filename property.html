About This Property

Welcome to an exceptional 2-bedroom apartment in the prestigious Nile Gate Tower, one of New Cairo's most coveted addresses. Positioned on the 24th floor, this fully furnished residence commands breathtaking panoramic views of the iconic New Cairo skyline and the shimmering Arabian Gulf.

The apartment has been finished to the highest premium standards with marble flooring throughout, a bespoke Italian kitchen, and floor-to-ceiling glass that floods every room with natural light. The open-plan living and dining area flows seamlessly onto a generous wraparound balcony — ideal for entertaining or enjoying the sunset over the sea.

Each bedroom is generously proportioned with built-in wardrobes, en-suite bathrooms featuring rain showers and designer fixtures. The master suite enjoys direct sea views, creating a truly hotel-like experience in the comfort of your own home.

Nile Gate offers an unrivalled collection of lifestyle amenities including an infinity pool, state-of-the-art fitness centre, spa, children's play areas, and a grand lobby with 24/7 concierge service. Residents enjoy direct access to the Nile Corniche, with world-class dining, boutique retail, and leisure all within walking distance.

This is a rare opportunity to own a landmark property in one of the world's most dynamic waterfront communities. Whether as a primary residence or a premium investment asset, Marina Gate consistently delivers strong rental yields and long-term capital appreciation.
